Connecting a Third-Party Form Service

If you prefer to deploy an interactive contact form on a static GitHub Pages site, we recommend integrating an external form-processing endpoint such as Formspree, Basin, or Formkeep. These services provide safe, spam-filtered POST endpoints without requiring an active server infrastructure.

When configuring your service, simply set the form's action attribute to your designated endpoint URL and include your honeypot spam protection.
