Transparency & Trust

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy outlines how SyntaxNest respects your privacy and handles visitor data.

1. Overview

SyntaxNest ("we", "our", or "the website") is a static educational technology blog hosted on GitHub Pages. We believe in transparent, minimal data practices and do not collect sensitive personal data, require user account creation, or process financial payments.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require you to register or submit personal details to read our tutorials and guides. If you choose to contact us via email, we only retain the email address and message contents you provide to respond to your inquiry.

3. Automatically Collected Information & Web Hosting

Because SyntaxNest is hosted on GitHub Pages (operated by GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft Corporation), standard web server access logs may be recorded automatically by GitHub’s infrastructure. These logs typically include your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser user-agent, operating system, referring URL, and the timestamp of page requests. These logs are maintained by GitHub for infrastructure security, network integrity, and abuse prevention according to the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

SyntaxNest does not currently set tracking cookies. Our client-side search functionality operates entirely inside your web browser’s memory using vanilla JavaScript without transmitting search queries to any remote servers.

5. Analytics

SyntaxNest may use privacy-preserving, aggregate analytics in the future to understand which articles are most helpful to readers (such as page views, country-level geography, and device types). Any analytics tool used will be configured to anonymize IP addresses and avoid intrusive cross-site tracking.

6. Advertising & Future Google AdSense Integration

SyntaxNest is currently operated as an advertisement-free educational platform. In the future, we may partner with third-party advertising networks, such as Google AdSense, to help support website operating costs.

When advertising networks are integrated:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to SyntaxNest and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users will be able to opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through aboutads.info.

This policy will be promptly updated to reflect active ad networks as soon as advertising is officially deployed.

7. Third-Party Services & External Links

Our guides frequently link to external developer resources, official documentation (such as Python.org, MDN Web Docs, GitHub documentation), and open-source code repositories. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or cookie policies of third-party websites. We encourage you to review the privacy statements of any external sites you visit.

8. Data Retention & Security

We do not store or maintain user databases. Any direct email correspondence is securely retained only as long as necessary to address your feedback or question, after which it is archived or deleted.

9. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area under GDPR and California under CCPA/CPRA), you have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to request deletion of any correspondence you have sent us. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technical enhancements, hosting changes, or future advertising partnerships. Revisions will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email us at contact.syntaxnest@example.com or reach out via our GitHub repository.
