Transparency & Trust

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy outlines how SyntaxNest respects your privacy and handles visitor data.

1. Overview

SyntaxNest ("we", "our", or "the website") is a static educational technology website hosted on GitHub Pages. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with visits to SyntaxNest, reader inquiries, and any future advertising services that may be integrated in accordance with applicable policies.

At the time of this policy, SyntaxNest does not operate an active analytics service, account system, or advertising network on the website. We keep the site lightweight and privacy-conscious by default, while remaining prepared for future advertising disclosures if a Google AdSense integration is enabled on the site.

2. Information Voluntarily Provided by Visitors

We do not require account registration or personal profile creation to access our content. If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message content, and any supporting context relevant to a question or correction request.

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry, address a technical concern, or maintain a helpful reader communication channel.

3. Contact Email

The current SyntaxNest contact email is nestsyntax@gmail.com. If you contact us via that address, we may retain the message and the contact details you provide so that we can reply and maintain a record of the communication as needed.

4. Hosting and Technical Data Processing

SyntaxNest is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may automatically record standard web server logs related to requests sent to the website. These logs can include your IP address, browser/user-agent string, referring URL, date and time of the request, and technical details about the request itself.

GitHub processes this information for service security, abuse prevention, reliability, and infrastructure operations. For more information, please review the GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Automatically Collected Technical Information

When you visit SyntaxNest, the website may automatically receive technical information from your browser and network. This can include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring page or URL
  • Request timestamps
  • Page path and general access metadata

This information is primarily used to maintain site reliability, diagnose technical issues, understand traffic patterns, and protect the website from misuse.

6. Cookies, Local Storage, and Site Functionality

SyntaxNest does not currently set advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or marketing cookies. The website’s search experience is implemented in the browser using standard JavaScript and does not transmit search queries to any third-party server.

We also do not currently use local storage or session storage for behavioral tracking or profiling. Any future use of cookies or storage technologies would be disclosed in this policy and only implemented when strictly necessary for a feature or required by a platform integration.

7. Search Functionality

The search feature on SyntaxNest runs locally in the browser and filters through existing article metadata. Search behavior is not used to create a user profile or track readers across external websites.

8. Analytics

SyntaxNest does not currently operate active analytics software such as Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Clarity, or other third-party measurement tools. If we later add a privacy-conscious analytics provider, we will update this policy and the implementation details to reflect the change.

9. Advertising and Google AdSense

As of the current site configuration, SyntaxNest does not display active advertisements or operate a live AdSense script. No advertising cookies are currently being used for ad serving on the site.

If Google AdSense is later enabled, this policy will be updated to reflect the active configuration. In that case, Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve ads based on users' visits to SyntaxNest and/or other websites.

Google advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to SyntaxNest and other sites on the internet. Third-party vendors, including Google, may place or read cookies on users' browsers to provide advertising-related services.

We may also disclose the current advertising configuration in the presence of a valid Google AdSense implementation, including whether the site is configured for personalized advertising or non-personalized advertising based on the actual account setup.

10. Google Advertising Information and User Controls

If advertising is enabled in the future, Google and its advertising partners may use information such as cookies, IP addresses, and other identifiers to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. Relevant disclosures and opt-out tools are available through Google and industry resources.

  • Google Ads Settings: https://www.google.com/settings/ads
  • Google advertising policy disclosure: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Industry opt-out information: https://www.aboutads.info
  • Digital Advertising Alliance consumer choice resources: https://optout.aboutads.info/?c=2&lang=EN

Users may also control ad personalization through browser settings and privacy tools provided by ad networks and operating systems where available.

11. Third-Party Advertising Vendors and Data Sharing

If advertising is later added, third-party vendors, including Google, may place or read cookies and other identifiers on this site when consent or eligibility requirements are met. These technologies may be used to measure ad performance, limit repeated advertising, and serve content relevant to a visitor's interests based on prior browsing behavior.

The exact configuration depends on the final Google AdSense account settings and any applicable consent management mechanism. SyntaxNest will not claim that personalized ads are active unless that configuration is actually implemented and disclosed.

12. EEA / UK / Switzerland Considerations

Because SyntaxNest is accessible to a global audience, visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may be subject to applicable privacy and consent rules. If advertising technology is enabled in a way that requires consent, the site will only use a compliant implementation consistent with the actual AdSense and consent configuration in place.

At present, no Google-certified consent management platform is active because no AdSense/advertising implementation is currently configured on this website.

13. Third-Party Websites and External Links

SyntaxNest links to external resources such as official documentation, open-source repositories, and technical references. These external websites have their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or cookie use of third-party websites, and we encourage readers to review those policies before engaging with those services.

14. Data Retention

We do not maintain a user database or marketing profile system for the public reading experience. Email correspondence is retained only as long as necessary to respond to a question, correction request, or other communication. If no longer needed, we may delete or archive the message according to operational best practices.

GitHub Pages server logs are retained in accordance with GitHub's operational and security policies. We do not use those logs for marketing or profiling on this website.

15. Security

We take reasonable technical and operational measures to help protect the website and any direct communications from unauthorized access, misuse, or accidental disclosure. However, no website or email communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure against all threats.

16. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding personal data, including the right to request access, correction, restriction, or deletion of information you have provided to us. If you have sent correspondence to SyntaxNest and would like it reviewed or deleted, please contact us at nestsyntax@gmail.com.

17. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, hosting environment, legal requirements, or any future advertising integrations. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.

18. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your interactions with SyntaxNest, please contact us at nestsyntax@gmail.com.
